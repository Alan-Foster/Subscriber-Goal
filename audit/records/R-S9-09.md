If the post-create trigger body has no post id, the route returns 400 and logs nothing. The invalid mod-action branch, by contrast, logs internal_trigger_failed.

A change in the trigger payload shape would silently stop community post activity from being recorded. Onboarding reads that activity (see #245), so the effect would show up far from the cause.
