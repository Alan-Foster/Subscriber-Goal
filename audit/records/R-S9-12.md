# R-S9-12: The emergency goal fallback is never saved, so it fires and logs an error every minute

**Problem · suspected** · criticality 4/10 · areas 9.03, 9.06, 13.06 · paths C5-queued-post-update

## 1. Current Implementation

When a queued goal post has no usable goal in Redis, the updater computes an emergency goal from the subscriber count, renders the post with it, and logs subscriber_goal_emergency_fallback at error. Nothing persists the recovered goal or stops the cycle.

## 2. Problem and evidence

The r/SubGoal logs on 2026-11-27 show the same subscriber_goal_emergency_fallback for t3_1xstm2j every minute (part of R-W6-01). Viewers see a plausible but invented goal.
